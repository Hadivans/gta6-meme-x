A GTA 6 meme for the make.fun bounty [Post a GTA 6 meme on X](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01): a "WANTED" poster for the make.fun ghost, the prime suspect in the theft of 13 years of our lives waiting for GTA 6 — last seen playing it in Leonida, with a reward of real money on make.fun.

The meme is a Vice City sunset street with the ghost front and center, five wanted stars, and one clear gag a GTA fan gets in a second. The picture was composed in the build session: an AI-made Vice City street, the real make.fun ghost mark from the press kit, and crisp vector text drawn on top so every word stays complete and legible at X timeline size.

## The entry

The entry is the X post itself. This repository hands in the post's address in `POST.json`:

```json
{"url": "https://x.com/<handle>/status/<id>"}
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` to the grader's output directory — that is all it has to do. The house reads the post from X (its first picture and its text) and an AI judge compares the posts two at a time on make.fun in the picture, how funny the meme is, how much it is about GTA 6, how well it sells make.fun, how short and readable it is, how original it is, and how clean the picture is. The top 50 posts share $500.
